Unlockt – Instagram Saved Vault & Bulk Media Exporter

Unlockt is a 100% private, local-first, open-source suite to bulk download, export, search, and permanently archive your entire Instagram Saved collection (Reels, Carousels, Photos, Audio).

  • No password required – uses your active Chromium browser session
  • 1-Click HTML5 Canvas photo collage generator (2x1 to 5x2 grid layouts)
  • Custom 9:16 vertical Reels video player with HTTP 206 Range streaming
  • AI Semantic Search across captions, hashtags, and creator handles
  • 100% offline – Zero telemetry, zero external tracking
