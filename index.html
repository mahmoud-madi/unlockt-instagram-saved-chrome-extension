Frequently Asked Questions

How does Unlockt download Instagram saved posts without asking for my password?
Unlockt operates as a Manifest V3 Chrome Extension inside your active browser session. It reads Instagram's internal GraphQL endpoints using the secure session cookies already present in your browser, keeping your account 100% safe.
Can Unlockt export multi-slide Instagram carousel posts?
Yes! Unlockt unpacks every slide in carousel posts, saving every individual high-resolution photo and video alongside individual slide captions and creator tags.
Does it preserve full HD audio and video quality for Reels?
Yes, Unlockt grabs direct MP4 video streams at their original resolution and bitrate with full AAC stereo audio tracks, completely free of watermarks or extra compression.
Is Unlockt completely free and open source?
Yes. Unlockt is licensed under the permissive MIT License. You can inspect the entire source code, run it completely offline, or contribute on GitHub.
